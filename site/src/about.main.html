Divicious Deli & Coffee Shop

A New York style deli with a hint of Cuban-infused flavor, on the corner of E Main St. across from Munn Park.

Divicious Deli & Coffee Shop is a New York Style deli with a hint of Cuban-infused flavor. This lively deli, located on the corner of E Main St. across from Munn Park, has provided a warm and welcoming environment to all who have entered.

From our tasty traditional Cuban sandwich to a wonderfully warm Cafe con Leche, Divicious Deli brings that live Cuban culture to the Lakeland community.

Businesswoman Yohansi Santana built Divicious Deli & Coffee Shop with a vision to improve the quality of food and culture in Lakeland. She has dedicated years to the shop, providing high-quality food, drinks and customer service so every customer has an exceptional experience.

Come enjoy our cozy, friendly and welcoming environment, with food and drinks that will have you craving more.

“There is nothing more gratifying than creating new relationships within the Lakeland community — we invite each customer to be a part of The Divicious Family.”
